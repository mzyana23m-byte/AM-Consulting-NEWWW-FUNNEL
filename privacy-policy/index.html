2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • When you book a call. Your name, email address and anything else you type into the booking form, including your phone number and details about your business if the form asks for them. Bookings are handled through our GoHighLevel booking calendar and qualification form.
  • During a consultation. Notes we take about your business, your offer and your numbers. Calls are held on Zoom.
  • When you email us. Whatever you choose to send, and your email address.

Information collected automatically

  • Standard server and log information from our host, including IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you requested and the time of the request.
  • The page you arrived from, and any campaign parameters in the link you clicked, such as utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content, utm_term and advertising click identifiers including fbclid.
  • A random internal identifier we generate in your browser, in the form am_ followed by a random string. It is not linked to your name and is used only to connect a visit to a later booking.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for and do not want health information, medical records, patient information or other sensitive categories of data. Several of our clients work in health sectors. We work on their marketing systems, not on their patient data. Please do not send us patient or client health information.

4. Cookies and advertising

What happens when you arrive depends on where you are.

  • United States, and Canada outside Quebec. We load the Meta Pixel when you arrive and record that a browser viewed the page. You do not have to click anything, and we do not show a cookie popup. You can switch it off at any time on our Privacy choices page.
  • Quebec, and everywhere else. Nothing loads. No advertising technology runs and no advertising cookie is set unless you switch measurement on yourself on our Privacy choices page.
  • If we cannot tell where you are, we treat you as the second group and load nothing.

To decide which applies, our host tells the page your country, and your province if you are in Canada. We keep that in a cookie for one day. It holds a code such as US or CA-ON. We do not store your IP address for this purpose.

When it loads, the Meta Pixel, dataset ID 2124222655634274, tells Meta that a browser visited a page on our site so we can measure whether our advertising works. Meta acts as an independent controller of that information under its own terms. You can read Meta's privacy policy at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

We have deliberately not switched on Meta's Advanced Matching feature, which would send hashed contact details such as your email address to Meta. We do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, call notes or anything you tell us about your business to Meta.

We honour the Global Privacy Control browser signal. If your browser sends that signal, advertising measurement stays off wherever you are, even if it would otherwise load automatically and even if a preference was previously saved on this device. An opt-out you have saved also overrides automatic loading.

Where advertising measurement is running, we store your campaign parameters and the internal identifier described above in your browser's local storage. Where it is not running, those details stay in memory for the page view and are never written to your device. Switching measurement off deletes anything already stored. That information stays on your device unless you book a call, in which case the campaign parameters and the internal identifier travel with your booking so we can tell which campaign it came from. We never put your name, email address or phone number into a tracking link.

We do not sell personal information for money. Allowing advertising cookies may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising, or as "targeted advertising", under some United States state privacy laws. Turning the setting off on our Privacy choices page, or sending a Global Privacy Control signal, stops it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and leads who do not become clients: deleted twelve months after our last interaction with you. This deletion is part of our routine process, not something you have to ask for.
  • Clients: for the length of the engagement, then as long as we need for tax, accounting and legal record keeping. In Canada that is generally six years.
  • Website server logs: for the period our host retains them.
  • Advertising cookies: according to the lifetime Meta sets, or until you clear them or withdraw consent.

7. Your rights

If you are in Canada. Under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, and under Quebec's Law 25 if you are in Quebec, you may ask us for access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it if it is wrong, and withdraw a consent you previously gave. You may also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or to your provincial regulator.

If you are in the United States. Depending on your state, you may have the right to know what we collect, to get a copy, to correct it, to have it deleted, and to opt out of targeted advertising or of the sharing of your information for cross-context behavioural advertising. You will not be treated differently for exercising these rights. To opt out of advertising measurement, use our Privacy choices page or send a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser.

How to make a request. Email aymanshoeib18@gmail.com and tell us what you want. We will confirm we received it and respond within thirty days. We may need to ask a question or two to confirm who you are, and we will not use those answers for anything else. You can ask an authorised agent to make a request for you.

Our service providers are largely based in the United States, so your information may be stored or processed there and may be accessible to authorities under the laws of that country.

9. Children
